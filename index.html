Questions

Straight answers

Can I get cover with an existing condition?

Often yes. Some insurers cover it after a waiting period or for a small extra cost. I tell you honestly which ones will accept you.

Do I need insurance for my visa?

O-A retirement visas need inpatient cover of at least ฿3,000,000 (about US$85,000). I arrange the certificate immigration asks for.

What happens when I go to hospital?

Show your card and the hospital bills the insurer directly. If anything goes wrong, call me and I deal with it.

Is there a fee for your help?

No. Insurers pay my commission, so the comparison and claims help cost you nothing.
